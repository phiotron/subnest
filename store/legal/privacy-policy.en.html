Subnest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

This policy explains how the mobile app Subnest: Subscription Tracker ("Subnest", "the app") for Android and iOS handles your information. Subnest is published by Phiotron ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal data. Subnest has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device. We have no server that stores your data and we cannot see it.
  • The internet is used only to download currency exchange rates. No information about you or your subscriptions is sent.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Backup files are created only when you ask, and you decide where to keep them.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores what you enter in its private storage on your device, for example:

  • subscriptions (service name, price, currency, billing cycle, renewal and trial dates, category, number of people you share a plan with, notes, the payment-method label you type, cancellation link);
  • payment history you confirm (paid / skipped periods and amounts);
  • settings (main currency, reminder time, theme, language) and the last downloaded exchange rates.

This data never leaves your device through Subnest, except when you yourself export or share a backup file (see below). You can delete individual items in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it.

Device backups: if you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Google backup on Android or iCloud Backup on iPhone), the operating system may include the app's data in that backup. This is managed by Google or Apple under their own terms and your device settings; we have no access to it.

Internet access: exchange rates

To convert prices into your main currency, the app downloads current exchange rates (about once a day, when you are online) from a public exchange-rate service: ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com).

  • The request only asks for the latest rates. It does not contain your subscriptions, amounts, notes, settings, or any identifier created by the app.
  • As with any connection on the internet, the service necessarily receives technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request. It processes this under its own privacy policy; we do not receive it.
  • You can keep using the app offline. It then uses the last downloaded rates, or rates you set yourself.

Notifications

Renewal and free-trial reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled and shown by your device's operating system. We do not use a push-notification server, and no notification content is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your system settings.

Backup and export files

When you choose "Export backup (JSON)" or "Export spreadsheet (CSV)", the app creates a file with your data and opens your system's share/save sheet. You choose where it goes (for example your files, a cloud drive or an email). We never receive these files. They are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust. When you restore, the app reads only the file you pick.

Links to websites

The app may open web pages in your browser, such as a service's "how to cancel" page or this policy. Those websites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Contacting us

If you send us feedback by email, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, do not share it, and will delete it on request.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show renewal and trial reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarms (Android, if requested) — so reminders arrive at the time you chose.
  • Internet — only to download exchange rates.
  • Files — the app only accesses a file you pick in the system file picker when restoring a backup.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or bank accounts.

Children

Subnest is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Data-protection laws (such as the GDPR in Europe, the CCPA in California and Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP) give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Because we do not collect or store your data, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete; you control everything directly in the app. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

We do not sell or share personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by your device's security (for example your screen lock). Exchange rates are downloaded over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Material changes (for example adding any data collection) will also be announced in the app's release notes before they take effect.
